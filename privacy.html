Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This page explains what information the EquityLens demo site handles.

What we collect

When you use the contact form or request a demo, your name, email and message are sent to our server so we can reply. They are delivered to the team by email (or held in our hosting provider’s request logs) and are not used for marketing or shared.

Questions you ask in AI research are sent to our server to search the indexed filings. They are not stored after the answer is returned.

Sign-up and sign-in are simulated: those details never leave your browser. Files you “upload” in AI research stay in your browser tab and are not sent anywhere.

Stored on your device

The site saves one preference in your browser’s local storage: your light or dark theme choice (el-theme). You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have a message you sent deleted, use the contact form. Clearing your browser storage removes the saved theme preference.

Changes

If what we collect changes — for example if real accounts are added — this page will be updated first.
